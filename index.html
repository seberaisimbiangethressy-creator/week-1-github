Tomato & Basil Pasta

A comforting bowl of pasta with ripe tomatoes, fresh basil, and Parmesan.

Prep and cook: 25 minutes   Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • 200 g dried pasta
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, chopped
  • 400 g ripe tomatoes, chopped
  • A handful of fresh basil
  • Parmesan, salt, and pepper

How to make it

  1. Boil the pasta in salted water until tender. Save a little pasta water, then drain.
  2. Warm the olive oil in a pan. Add garlic, then tomatoes. Cook until soft and saucy.
  3. Add the pasta and a splash of pasta water to the sauce. Stir in basil, season, and serve with Parmesan.

Tips

Use the ripest tomatoes you can find. Add a pinch of chilli flakes for heat.
